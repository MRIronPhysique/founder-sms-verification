Founder SMS Privacy Policy

MR Iron Physique LLC uses the founder’s verified business number, consent and withdrawal records, and SMS delivery records to send and administer internal alerts. Messages use enrollment references and operational status. New-enrollment and founder-verified payment alerts also include the package name and amount. Customer contact information, health information and bank account details are kept out of SMS. Detailed enrollment information stays in the restricted application.

Twilio and mobile carriers process the recipient number, message content and delivery information to deliver messages and handle opt-out. The company stores signed consent privately. Once hosted, its application provider will process delivery records on the company’s behalf. Operational email alerts are sent separately via the authorized email provider. The company does not sell SMS consent or share mobile information for third-party marketing.

The planned hosted service will restrict access to authorized personnel, use HTTPS, and protect provider credentials in the host’s secret store. Provider delivery logs are subject to provider and carrier policies.

Proposed retention for owner approval: keep signed consent and withdrawal evidence while enrolled and for 24 months after withdrawal. Keep routine application delivery logs for 90 days. Retain a minimal suppression record while needed to prevent further sends. Longer retention may apply if required for a specific legal obligation or dispute. These periods require implementation and verification before publication.

Contact mrironphysique@gmail.com to request access, correction, or deletion of SMS records, or to ask how records are handled.
